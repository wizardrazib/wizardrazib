# Mini-Market Math Missions

100 Fun Math & Logic Puzzles for Kids Ages 7-9

Mira West

## Mission 1: Fruit & Veggie Stand

Shelf: Apple 15¢, Banana 30¢, Carrot 50¢, Pear 85¢

### Puzzle 1

Buy one apple and one carrot. What is their total cost?

Answer: 65

15 + 50 = 65¢. Add one price for each item.

### Puzzle 2

Buy the same two items for 65¢. Pay 100¢. How much change comes back?

Answer: 35

100 − 65 = 35¢. Check: 65 + 35 = 100.

### Puzzle 3

The receipt lists one banana and one pear. Fill in the total.

Answer: 115

30 + 85 = 115¢. Both receipt lines are included.

### Puzzle 4

Choose exactly TWO DIFFERENT items that cost 80¢ altogether. Write both names.

Answer: Banana + Carrot

30 + 50 = 80¢. No other pair of different items on this shelf has that sum.
