# Mini-Market Math Missions — competitor and sample-access analysis

Research session: 4 November 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). Author: Mira West. Approved niche: Grade 2–3 market math/logic, ages 7–9, Amazon.com US, $0 external production budget. Planned full edition: 108 pages, 8.5 × 11 inches, black ink, no bleed. Exact approved subtitle: 100 Fun Math & Logic Puzzles for Kids Ages 7-9. Only eight numbered sample puzzles are produced here; the full 100-puzzle count is not yet fulfilled. No claim of guaranteed KDP compliance, educational efficacy, organic sales or profitability.

## Method and limits

Targeted search discovery, followed by the publisher's Spectrum product page and its public Look Inside link. No interactive browser tool is available. The Look Inside endpoint returned HTTP 500 through the web reader. Publisher page gallery links were visible but image pixels were not retrieved/visually inspected. Do not confuse gallery captions with sample inspection. No paywall, CAPTCHA, login or unauthorized copy was bypassed. No copied competitor artwork or text is used in our original sample.

This is a provisional comparator analysis, not completed interior-sample validation. No competitor interior pages were visually inspected, and no representative customer-review sample was read. Further lawful sample access or owner-supplied excerpts are needed before making comparative interior-quality claims. No Amazon overall rank screen or paid/free rank comparisons were completed; high demand and low competition remain UNKNOWN.

## Original sample brief

Two facing-page spreads, four pages total. They are not final book folios: layout starts left/right to simulate eventual even/odd facing pages. Four pages contain eight distinctly numbered puzzles (not sixteen by counting each answer blank). A separate two-page illustrated answer key is a review companion and will eventually belong at the back of the 108-page book, not inside these activity spreads.

- Spread 1: observe shelf prices, add two prices, check a receipt, calculate change.
- Spread 2: repeated-addition inventory, missing receipt line, unique two-item budget match, change with a count-up explanation.
- Integer cents throughout; all market prices fictional, tax included, no discounts or additional fees. Use cent sign rather than requiring decimal arithmetic. Explain 100¢ = $1 and 200¢ = $2.
- Local data drives shelf tags, receipt lines and answer key. Independent reference calculations check results. Enumeration checks the two-item budget task has exactly one valid unordered pair of different products.
- Mission Complete color-in star on each facing spread; generous response boxes, strong black outlines and no color-dependent clues.

Review gate: author reviews voice, activity difficulty, legibility and illustration style. Educator/child usability review still needed; no full-book drafting, cover generation or retail submission until sample direction is accepted. The exact title/subtitle are retained as requested; 100-puzzle marketing claims must be verified against the eventual finished book.
