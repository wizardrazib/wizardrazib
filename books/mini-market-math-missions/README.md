# Mini-Market Math Missions — review sample

Author: Mira West
Subtitle: **100 Fun Math & Logic Puzzles for Kids Ages 7-9**

- `sample-4-pages.pdf`: four activity pages, arranged as two facing-page spreads.
- `sample-answer-key.pdf`: two separate illustrated answer pages for review.
- `sample-preview.png`: reduced-size contact sheet; print the PDF at 100% for review.
- `RESEARCH.md`: comparator facts, sources, and sample-access limitations.
- `validation.json`: local arithmetic and PDF checks.
- `project.json`: author-confirmed full-book requirements.

This is eight numbered puzzles, not a finished 100-puzzle/108-page book.
The proposed full book must contain exactly 100 distinct numbered puzzles,
and its answer key and front matter must fit inside 108 total interior pages.
No cover or full-book manuscript was created in this sample step.

All sample artwork is original local vector drawing. Text and artwork are
AI-generated through local scripts; no paid content-generation service was used.
Receipt arithmetic, repeated additions, change and budget-pair uniqueness are
checked in integer cents. A human educator and child-reader review is pending.
Publisher preview access failed; no competitor interior pages were inspected.

Rebuild with Python 3.11+, ReportLab, Pillow and PyMuPDF:

    python build_sample.py

Requires local DejaVu Sans regular/bold at the font paths in the script.
The sample uses mirrored inner margins (50 pt) to simulate facing pages.
No bleed. No printer marks. Preserve original margins when printing.
Reproduction of typography/layout does not certify KDP acceptance.
